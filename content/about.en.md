This is my personal website, blog and lab to test all new features of Swift.

## MindChess

[MindChess](https://mindchess.app) is my iOS app: a daily chess gym and study companion. You train board vision, memory and calculation in 60-second drills, sharpen your tactics, play bots with their own style, and bring your Lichess and Chess.com games in to see what to improve. It's built with SwiftUI and The Composable Architecture, and a good part of what I write here comes from building it.

- Website: [mindchess.app](https://mindchess.app)
- [Download it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6745103930)
